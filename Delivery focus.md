Pick one **delivery focus**: the free thing you give away. Examples: webinars, videos, newsletters.

## Rules
- Everything else you post revolves around the webinar. Leave "breadcrumbs" that lead to it.
- Your biggest task, once you have a customer base (people who pay and vouch for you), is to move people toward the delivery focus.
- A useful number to develop: webinars per sale. See [[Webinars per sale]].
