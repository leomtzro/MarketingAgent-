Narrowcasting, not broadcasting: talk to your fans, not everyone.

## Focus on only 2 numbers
1. **Conversion rate:** see [[Close rate]] and [[Sales funnel metrics]].
2. **NPS / customer satisfaction.** NPS (Net Promoter Score) measures how likely customers are to recommend you.

## Start with one question
Who are your fans? See [[Second-slide customers]] for how to find them, [[Customer scoresheet]] to rank them and [[Customer avatar]] to understand them.
