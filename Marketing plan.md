Takeaway: what the Chief Marketing Officer does to carry out the [[Lead generation plan]]. Marketing makes the clinic owner interested and gives Sales warm conversations. Changes to the plan are made in Strategy first.

## Orders from Strategy
1. **Lead magnet:** finish the free "review of your last 20 messages" and test its name before launch. Test names by asking people in our list, a post and a poll, no ads yet ([[Lead magnet checklist]]).
2. **Website:** get it online as the place where a clinic owner requests the review: [[Website launch checklist]].
3. **Audience:** write the clinic-owner avatar once Leo picks the clinic type ([[Customer avatar]], [[Narrowcasting]]).
4. **Proof:** collect case studies from the first founding clinics, with their written consent, to raise the perceived likelihood of success ([[Warm outreach]]).
5. **Research, free:** watch competitors' ads and posts, write down the top 50 you can find, write 10 versions of our own ([[Make ads]]). No spend.
6. **Content and ads:** not yet. Start when Strategy says so. Rules then: content made for strangers ([[Content as lead getter]]), give in public and ask in private ([[Content monetization and scaling]]).

## Rules
- Health claims, patient photos and testimonials follow Meta's health-ad rules and need consent. Check before publishing.
- Scarcity and urgency use real capacity and real dates only.
- Nothing promises a result we cannot back.

## Hand-off to Sales
Anyone who requests the review goes to Sales the same day with where they came from.

## Numbers to report each week
Review requests, visits to the website, requests per visit. Benchmarks: `unknown`.
