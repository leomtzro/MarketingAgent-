# Qué publicar en X

**Lectura del canal.** Los compradores (dueños de clínica en Tijuana) no están en X.
Están en Facebook, Instagram y WhatsApp. X sirve para otra cosa: que el censo viaje
y que Meridian se vea como el despacho que mide, no como el que promete.

- **Idioma: inglés.** Los clientes no están aquí de todos modos, y en inglés el
  alcance es varias veces mayor. El contenido en español va a IG y FB.
- **Costo: bajo.** Una vez a la semana. Si pide más, no lo damos.
- **Regla intacta:** ninguna clínica se nombra. Si alguien pregunta cuáles, no se dice.
  Esa negativa, en público, es publicidad mejor que el dato.

---

## 1. El hilo principal (el activo)

Publicar una vez. Fijarlo en el perfil.

> **1/** We messaged 40 medical clinics in Tijuana pretending to be patients.
>
> Every one of them pays for Meta ads.
>
> 24 hours later, 6 in 10 had not replied at all.
>
> What we found was worse than slow service. 🧵

> **2/** First, the number that matters.
>
> Of the 26 we could test, 19 failed.
>
> But only 3 of those failures were "slow."
>
> 7 of them — 37% — never received the message at all.

> **3/** Their WhatsApp button was broken.
>
> The ad runs. The budget is spent. A patient taps the button and it opens nothing.
>
> No error reaches the clinic. No alert. Nothing shows up in any report.
>
> It just looks like a quiet month.

> **4/** One clinic has been running the same ad since October 2024.
>
> Thirteen months.
>
> Broken button the entire time.

> **5/** Then there's the part nobody fixes because nobody loses sleep over it.
>
> 31 of 40 had no usable website. 24 had nothing at all.
>
> The ad sends a stranger to a profile. A profile has no price, no hours, no map.
>
> So they go find those somewhere else.

> **6/** We assumed size would protect the bigger accounts.
>
> It doesn't.
>
> Of the clinics with 5,000+ followers, 70% still had no usable site.
>
> The biggest account in the set — 17,000 followers — has no website and never replied.

> **7/** None of this is a marketing problem.
>
> It's a plumbing problem that marketing gets blamed for.
>
> The ads work. People click. The path after the click is broken, and the people
> paying for the ads have never walked it.

> **8/** If you run ads for anything, do this today:
>
> Open your own ad on your phone, on mobile data, not wifi.
>
> Tap your own button.
>
> That's it. That's the whole audit. It takes two minutes and almost nobody does it.

> **9/** We're not naming a single clinic. Not here, not anywhere, not if you ask.
>
> If you run a clinic in Tijuana and you want to know whether you were in the 40 —
> just test your own button. You'll know in ten seconds.

## 2. Publicaciones sueltas

Una por semana. Cada una se sostiene sola.

> 37% of the failures we found weren't slowness.
>
> They were a broken button.
>
> "Reply faster" is a lecture. "Your button is broken" is a favor.
>
> Only one of those gets answered.

---

> A clinic has been paying for the same ad since October 2024.
>
> Thirteen months, same creative, broken contact button.
>
> Nobody is asleep at the wheel. There's just nobody at the wheel.

---

> Everyone audits the ad.
>
> Almost nobody audits what happens 3 seconds after the click.
>
> That's where we found 100% of the money going missing.

---

> We tested on mobile data, not office wifi.
>
> Two of the failures only appear on a cold connection.
>
> If you test your funnel from the same network that built it, you are not testing it.

---

> 23 of the 40 clinics post regularly.
>
> 31 of the 40 have no usable website.
>
> Somebody is doing the content. Nobody built anywhere to send it.

---

> The hardest thing to sell a business owner is a problem they can verify in ten seconds.
>
> Not because they doubt you.
>
> Because once they check, they can't unknow it — and that's the part they resent.

---

## 3. Qué NO publicar
- Nada de "contrátanos" ni ofertas. Aquí no se vende, se demuestra.
- Ningún nombre de clínica, ni capturas donde se alcance a leer uno.
- Nada de prometer resultados: no tenemos ni un caso todavía.
- Nada de quejarse del gremio ni de otras agencias. Se ve pequeño.
- No traducir el contenido de IG tal cual. Son dos audiencias distintas.

## 4. Cómo medir si vale la pena
Darle **seis semanas**. Si al final no hay ni una conversación con alguien útil
(un dueño de clínica, un socio, un cliente potencial de otra ciudad), cerrar el canal
sin ceremonia y poner esas horas en los mensajes directos.
