Give more free resources, in a professional and lasting form (PDF, newsletter), **in exchange for the person's email**.

## The thesis
If someone is not in our contacts and has not been to the webinar or heard of us, but still gives their email to get the information, they are probably desperate enough: they are in **pain**.

## What to measure
The number of people who leave their email for a guide and were not at the webinar or in our contacts. See [[Guide email capture from strangers]].
