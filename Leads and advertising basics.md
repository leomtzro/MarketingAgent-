Takeaway: leads come first, and a lot of leads makes it hard to be poor. Leads do not appear by themselves, so you force them to find you. That is advertising.

- Advertising = the process of making known. Leo's note calls it a "get out of jail free" card.
- $100M Offers answers "what to sell". $100M Leads answers "who to sell to".
- Problem: you are not getting as many leads as you want because you are not advertising enough.
- Solution: advertise more, advertise better, advertise in new ways. Goal is more, cheaper, better leads, reliably, from many places. See [[More Better New]].
- All else equal, 10x leads = 10x business. If you can get more, cheaper, better leads you never worry about one going away.
- An entrepreneur usually lacks the what, the who and the how.
- Lead = a person you can contact.
- Engaged lead (what we want) = a person interested in what you sell. Examples: replies to an email campaign, gives you contact info, follows you on social and you can contact them.
- Leo's observation: Alex explains his own business model so you know you are not being scammed or played. Copy that.
