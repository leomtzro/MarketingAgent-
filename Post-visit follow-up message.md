The ending of the patient experience. Sent about 24 hours after the visit. Spanish, adapted and untested.

## Message
"Hola María, gracias por venir hoy. El doctor pidió revisar tu cuidado en 3 días; te escribo el martes para saber cómo vas. Si algo te preocupa, responde aquí y una persona del consultorio te contesta hoy."

The doctor's instruction and the follow-up day come from the clinic; the agent never invents them.

## Rules
- Warm, short, one clear next step.
- Ask for a review only after the patient replies positively.
- No medical advice; anything about symptoms goes to staff.

## Recovery after a long wait (more than 20 minutes, threshold is our guess)
Send a short apology and offer a small gesture agreed with the clinic, such as priority for the next appointment. The gesture itself is `unknown` until the clinic decides.
