Takeaway: brand is a massive multiplier and content is more scalable than warm outreach, but it is competitive, takes time to master, and good content gets copied. Every content unit needs a hook, a retain and a reward.

## Hook: a reason to notice
Pick good topics: far past (life lessons), recent past (breakdown), present (real-time capture), trends (apply your lens), manufactured (idea to reality, least common, set a cadence such as 2 a month).

Pick good headlines, 7 kinds: recency, relevancy (nurses care about what affects nurses), celebrity, proximity, conflict, unusual, ongoing.

Match the format that rewarded them before: each platform has norms (IG photos and lifestyle, X info and news). Make content look like what they expect to reward them.

## Retain: keep their attention through curiosity
Lists ("28 ways to stay poor"), steps ("3 steps to a great hook"), stories ("My journey", "The time when I").

## Reward: satisfy the reason they came
Quality = how often it rewards the audience for the time it takes (value per second). You cannot guarantee reward but can raise the odds. The audience decides if it is good by matching or beating expectations. Success = the audience grows.

## Types
- Short: less skill, good to start. Leo's open item: work out how much to post, include "medium content", look at studies of attention ranges.
- Long: much more skill, you must retain someone for an hour.
- Make all content for strangers; reference old content as little as possible.
