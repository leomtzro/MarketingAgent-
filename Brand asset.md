Your brand asset is the answer to: **"Give me one good reason to do business with you."** Customers are looking for ONE THING.

It is not "it's fast" or "it's easy to use". It has to be:
1. **Distinguishing**
2. **Memorable**
3. **Powerful:** the thing people actually care about

## How to find it
Ask 10 people who already bought your product to give you the one real reason why they bought from you.

## Example: the TV mount
A company's marketing and packaging said 5 things: easy to assemble, lightweight, foldable, competitively priced, made of aircraft steel. When buyers were asked why they bought, they answered: "I don't know what aircraft steel is, but I want a TV mount made out of that."
