Takeaway: grow lead generation in this order: More, then Better, then New. Exhaust More and Better first.

- **More:** crank volume to max capacity. See [[Rule of 100]]. More ads mean better ads: Facebook found its top advertisers test ads 11 times more than average (Leo's note, as written in the book).
- **Better:** same effort, more leads. Test one thing per week per platform. Reasons: otherwise you never know what works, steps affect each other, one test at a time makes you prioritize, and the test must run long enough (use statistical significance) but not longer. Front to back: usually focus on the front (first impression) over the back (value or CTA), since conversion rate gives enough info.
- **New:** new places and new ways. If someone else in your market is getting customers that are not you, there is more to do. Move to New when you approach 30% of your market. Order: new placements (easy), new platform (harder), new Core Four channel (hardest).
