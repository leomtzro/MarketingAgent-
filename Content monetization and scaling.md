Takeaway: give in public, ask in private. The moment you start asking for money publicly is the moment you stop growing.

## Give:ask ratio
- It works through reciprocity. You know you have it when people ask you to push their brand.
- To just monetize: 3 to 4 posts per ad. To grow and monetize: give, give, give until they ask.
- Integrated ask: advertise inside every piece as long as you keep giving. A 30-second plug in a 60-minute video is a 119:1 ratio. Put the CTA right after a valuable moment.
- Intermittent ask: for short form. Make 10 give posts, promote in the 11th.

## Scaling
- Depth then width: one relevant platform, post regularly, max quality and quantity (short form up to 10 a day, long form about weekly), then add another platform. Pro: audiences compound faster with fewer resources. Con: less low-hanging fruit, single-channel dependence.
- Width then depth: post on a platform, move on to the next until relevant on all, then maximize both. Pro: broader audience faster. Con: more time and labor, often bad content everywhere.

## How Leo would do it
1. Find topics with the five ways in [[Content as lead getter]].
2. Twice a month, record about 30 short clips.
3. Same day, make 2 to 4 longer videos unpacking the posts with the most stories or impact.
4. Why: at some point ads are not the driver, long-form is. Find out how many customers saw long or short form before buying.

## 7 lessons
1. Switch "how to" to "how why": give advice, claim nothing.
2. We need reminding more than teaching.
3. Puddles, ponds, lakes, oceans: win the puddle first, be narrow.
4. Content creates tools for business people: list your "greatest hits", label each with the problem solved and the benefit.
5. Free content retains paying customers. People judge more off free content.
6. People do not have short attention spans, just higher standards. No such thing as too long, only too boring.
7. Avoid pre-scheduling: posting manually makes you hesitate and judge more objectively.

## First post and benchmarks
- First post can include an ask. Do not fear opinions.
- Benchmarks: total followers, and the rate of getting followers and reach. Growing = good, growing fast = better.
