Spend a day in your customer's life, then turn what you learn into a character the whole team can use.

## Spend a day in their life
Sam Walton's story: everyone said big retail in small-town America wouldn't work. So he spent Fridays in small towns with a John Deere hat and work boots, eating pie and having coffee with farmers. In his words: "Farmers designed my stores."

## Name them
Once you understand them enough, name them and create a character, or even better, a cardboard cut-out.

**Rule:** if your team cannot explain their ideas and processes to your avatar, you don't want to hear it.
