Takeaway: the website design (v3, Spanish, pesos, WhatsApp) exists on the design canvas but is not live. What blocks going live is mostly decisions and a few accounts only Leo can open, not code.

## A. Decisions (Leo), because the page has placeholders
- Price and pricing model, guarantee terms, founding terms, capacity number per month, start time.
- How we describe the delivery to clinic owners (what the team does and what the system does), then the FAQ answer "will patients know it is not my team".
- Which clinic type the page speaks to first.
- Real founder names, faces and story for the trust section.
See [[Open questions - 100M Offers notes]] and the handoff [[2026-11-03 0014 chat to design Website v3 feedback]].

## B. Name and identity
- Check that "Meridian" is free (IMPI trademark search). It is a placeholder name. `unknown` whether it is available.
- Business identity for invoices and the privacy notice: `unknown` whether it will be Leo as a person or a company.

## C. Accounts (Leo opens them; Jarvis cannot create accounts or enter payment details)
1. Buy the domain (.mx or .com). `unknown` whether one is bought.
2. Open a free account at a static-site host (Cloudflare Pages, Netlify or Vercel). The page is a single static site, so cost is the domain only.
3. A WhatsApp number for the business and the click-to-chat link on the page (no Meta approval needed for a link).
4. A business email on the domain (optional at first).

## D. Jarvis builds
- Turn the v3 canvas into final site code, mobile first, with the placeholders filled.
- A form or WhatsApp button for "request the free review", sending leads to one place Sales checks daily.
- Draft privacy notice (aviso de privacidad) for the form, to be reviewed by a lawyer. It is a draft, not legal advice.
- Check contrast and tap sizes flagged in the handoff.

## E. Before going live
- Every claim on the page is true on day one (no "answer every message", no Instagram, no invented numbers).
- Test on a phone: form, WhatsApp link, calculator.
- Send the lead somewhere a person reads within the reply promise.

## F. Go live
Point the domain to the host, check https works, send the first review request through it yourself.

## Optional, not needed to launch
Analytics, Meta pixel, search listing. Add after the first requests.
