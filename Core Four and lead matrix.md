Takeaway: there are only 4 ways one person can tell other people about anything. Two audiences times two kinds of communication.

- Audiences: people who know you (warm, you have permission to contact) and people who don't (cold, no permission).
- Communication: one-to-one (private, outbound: first contact happens in private) and one-to-many (public, inbound: contact publicly hoping it becomes one-to-one).

| | People who know you | People who don't know you |
|---|---|---|
| One-to-one | Warm outreach | Cold outreach |
| One-to-many | Free content | Paid ads |

Each channel has its own note: [[Warm outreach]], [[Content as lead getter]], [[Cold outreach]], [[Make ads]].

Order of effort in Leo's notes: warm outreach is the cheapest and most reliable. Content is more scalable but competitive and slow. Cold outreach needs no content and is hard to copy. Ads are the money lever. Growth path: [[More Better New]].
