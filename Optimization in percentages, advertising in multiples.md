Takeaway: optimization talks in percentages, advertising talks in multiples (X's).

- **Optimization (%):** works inside the known universe. You squeeze more out of people who already see you: a better caption, a faster reply, a higher conversion rate. The ceiling is 100% of that universe.
- **Advertising (X's):** expands the known universe. It puts you in front of people who did not know you existed, so results grow by 2x, 5x, 10x instead of a few percent.

## How we use it
1. When a number is stuck, ask first: is this a percentage problem (convert better) or a multiple problem (reach more people)?
2. Do not polish a page seen by 50 people. Fix reach first, then optimize what the bigger audience does.
3. Report the two separately: optimization wins as % change, advertising wins as audience multiples.

## Transfers to other domains
- Sales: improving a close rate is %, adding a new referral channel is X's.
- Careers: getting better at your current job is %, entering a bigger arena is X's.
- Agents: tuning a prompt is %, giving an agent a new channel or task is X's.
