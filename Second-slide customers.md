Your fans are the "second slide" customers.

## The story
A customer looks at the second slide and says, "Where have you been all my life? I'll buy it." The salesman keeps going. The CEO sends him to wait in the car. The salesman replies, "I have 18 more slides to go."

## How to find them
1. Ask your salespeople for the name of the easiest sale they ever had.
2. Ask customer service: "If a customer was going to tattoo the company's logo because they love it, who would it be?"
3. Build a 100-point profile: [[Customer scoresheet]].

## When you find one
Shut up once someone has already said yes on the second slide. The rule is also in [[Sales call rules]].
