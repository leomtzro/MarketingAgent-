## Frameworks used
- What x Who x When ([[Make ads]]): What more = dream, fast, easy, likely; what less = nightmare, slow, hard, risky. Who = themselves, family, friends, colleagues, rivals. When = past, present, future.
- Callout types ([[Make ads]]): labels, yes-questions, if-then, ridiculous results.
- [[Value equation]]: raise dream and likelihood, cut time and effort.
- [[Brand asset]]: distinguishing, memorable, powerful. One reason to choose us.
- MAGIC ([[Offer naming]]): magnetic, avatar, goal, interval, container word.

## Making it lasting: one metaphor everywhere
Memorability comes from repeating one odd idea across the whole profile, not from one line.
- Bio: exterminators / fumigation of empty chairs.
- Call to action: "Inspección gratis de tu Instagram ↓" (the free audit lead magnet, renamed as an inspection).
- Highlights: "Inspecciones", "Fumigaciones" (results), "Plagas" (common problems), "Equipo".
- Content series: "Plaga de la semana": one common clinic mistake per week.
