Takeaway: a design skill is a rulebook Claude reads before it builds, so every page follows the same strategy and visual system, and is not done until a checklist passes. Matches the next phase (website design).

**Two halves plus a checklist**
- **Strategy half.** Intake first: the one action, the offer, the buyer, top three objections, traffic source, proof. A fixed 12-section order from outcome headline to FAQ, guarantee and a final button matching the first. Copy rules: one offer, one audience, one button, real numbers instead of vague words.
- **Visual half.** One font, colors as hex codes, a short list of spacing values, one corner style, one motion curve, all in one section so a brand change is one edit.
- **Checklist.** Strategy, visuals, shipping basics (404 page, form validation, alt text, privacy and terms links, title and meta description, social image).

**Process rules**
- Claude interviews you and writes the skill, rather than you writing it.
- One skill per job. Ask for changes one section at a time, never a full rebuild for one change.
- Hero: three headline formulas ("{Outcome} without {pain}", "The {category} for {audience}", "Get {result} in {time}"), three stacked options, a verb-first button label.
- Browser check: render at phone, tablet and desktop width, screenshot, fix the top failures, up to three rounds, and never publish without a human yes.
- A campaign page gets noindex, an evergreen page gets search-ready title, description and FAQ.
